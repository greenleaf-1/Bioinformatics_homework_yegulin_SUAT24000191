# Week 5 DESeq2 interpretation

We analyzed 1,000 genes across 12 RNA-seq samples with a DESeq2 model that adjusted for batch while estimating the treated-versus-control effect. After requiring at least 10 counts in three samples, 989 genes remained. PCA of variance-stabilized counts showed complete separation by condition along PC1 (24.1% of variance), while batches were distributed within each condition, supporting a treatment-associated global expression shift rather than simple batch confounding. Using `padj < 0.05` and an apeglm-shrunken `|log2FoldChange| >= 1`, 60 genes were differentially expressed: 36 upregulated and 24 downregulated in treated samples. The volcano plot shows strong evidence on both sides, with Gene0035 among the most significant upregulated genes and Gene0100 among the strongest downregulated genes. Because the approved inputs contain synthetic gene identifiers without functional annotations, pathway-level biological claims cannot be made from this analysis alone.
